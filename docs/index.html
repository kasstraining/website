Training is personal.
Kass keeps it that way.

Kass is a training app for coaches and their clients. Every session, every weight, every note about your body is sealed between the two of you before it leaves your phone. The server that runs Kass can't read any of it — and neither can we.

Nobody's business but yours

A training log is a record of a body: what it can lift, where it's injured, what it weighs, when it's alone in a building. Kass treats it that way.

Sealed by default

Your log is locked on your phone before it's saved anywhere, with keys that never leave it. If someone broke into our servers tomorrow, they'd carry away noise.

The next session is always ready

Kass works out your next weights from what you actually lifted — including the session where everything went wrong. Walk in, tap Start, train.

Built for basement gyms

Signal comes and goes; your log doesn't. Kass keeps working offline, and a set, once tapped, is never lost.

Your history is yours for life

Change coaches and your log stays whole, because it belongs to the person who trained it. A new coach joins your story — they don't restart it.

For coaches

Write a programme once and give each client their own living copy — it adapts to how they actually train, without touching anyone else's. And no client can ever see another client's log: not because of a permission setting, but because each person's Kass is genuinely theirs, sealed to them alone.

Your programmes, your client list, and your notes stay in your own Kass, just as sealed as theirs.

How private is private?

Encrypted before it leaves your phone
Weights, reps, bodyweight, injuries, messages — all of it is sealed on your device. The server stores and delivers sealed records it cannot open.
No big database of everyone
Each person's Kass stands alone. There is no shared pool of training data to mine, sell, or leak — nothing readable exists to take.
AI only if you ask
Kass works completely without it. Switch it on and it's a disclosed guest — everything it writes is marked 🤖, and your name never leaves the building.
